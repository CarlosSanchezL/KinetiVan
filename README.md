# KinetiVan

**More energy. More freedom.**

🔗 **Live site:** https://carlossanchezl.github.io/KinetiVan/

KinetiVan is a kinetic flooring energy system for camper vans. Floor tiles capture the energy from every step you take inside your van and turn it into clean, usable power. That means no noise, no fuel and less reliance on generators, so you can stay off-grid longer.

This repository contains the landing page for the product, including an interactive demo of the KinetiVan app.

---

## How KinetiVan works

1. **Movement activates the floor tiles.** Each step presses a kinetic tile and generates between 2 and 8 W.
2. **Energy flows to the controller,** which manages and regulates the power.
3. **It's stored in the buffer battery,** so the energy is there when you need it.
4. **It's available through USB-C** to charge phones, laptops, lights and other devices.

A normal day of movement inside the van generates around **200–400 Wh** with an 8-tile kit.

## The KinetiVan App

The app lets you monitor and control your energy system in real time:

- **Battery level:** see your current charge and remaining power time.
- **Energy generated:** track how much power your movement produced today.
- **Kinetic tiles:** see which tiles are active and how much power is flowing.
- **USB-C output:** turn the output on or off and see how many watts your devices are using.
- **System status:** check that the tiles, controller and battery are all working correctly.

### Try the live demo
The website includes a working demo of the app inside a phone mockup:
- Go to the **Tiles** tab and **tap the tiles** to "walk." You'll see the battery go up.
- Turn on **"Simulate walking automatically"** to generate energy on its own.
- Tap the **USB-C output** card to turn it on or off. When it's on, the battery slowly drains.
- Check the **Battery** tab for stored energy, remaining time and system status.
