How it works

Turning your steps
into power.

KinetiVan uses kinetic floor tiles that capture energy from your movement. This energy is controlled, stored in a buffer battery and available through USB-C for your devices.

  1. 1 Movement activates the floor tiles
  2. 2 Energy flows to the controller
  3. 3 Stored in the buffer battery
  4. 4 Available through USB-C

How much energy can the tiles generate?

Each tile produces between 2 and 8 W per step depending on weight and pressure. A normal day of movement inside the van generates around 200–400 Wh with an 8-tile kit.

Can I install it myself?

Yes. The tiles click together and sit on top of your existing floor. The controller and battery connect with a single cable. Installation takes about 2 hours.

Does it work with solar panels?

Absolutely. The controller has an optional solar input, so both sources charge the same buffer battery.

What happens if a tile breaks?

The system is modular: you can replace a single tile without touching the rest. All kits include a 3-year warranty.

Is it waterproof?

Tiles are rated IP65 — resistant to dust, mud and splashes. The battery should stay in a dry area.
